[b]Version 0.15.0-dev.1[/b]
MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED

Sorting and Shopping collection now require knowledge of the work location by default: carry a completed matching paper ledger, or use a powered computer with a matching ledger CD loaded from accessible storage beside it. Shopping planning/editing, recipes and container configuration remain available without a ledger. Server sandbox options control the two requirements and nearby-computer range (default 10 tiles). Same-building computers are not distance limited; records must still cover the work location.

In Windows '93, load a real ledger CD from an adjacent desk/container, then run QUARTERMASTER.EXE. Loading/ejecting changes a server-owned binding, never the item. Logging off leaves the computer binding available for work around the building. Computer power, item presence and current access are rechecked for new grants. Carrying a CD alone does not qualify. Known paper/mounted media are protected from automatic movement, while manual native actions remain possible.

Completed native transfers stay completed after proof loss. Safe return/observation commands remain available, and new TAKE/PUT grants stop. Existing sorting/presets/priorities/physical ledger snapshots remain; no inventory data is deleted. Movement capability 10, Shopping 2 and Ledger 2 require matching server/client builds. Vanilla smaller native transfer groups and timing are unchanged. Native SP/dedicated validation pending; no package, deployment or upload is claimed by this note.
