Inventory and selected-container Sort work without a supply ledger. Room/Building Sort and Shopping collection require fresh location knowledge by default.

New server Ledger modes: periodic updates with live statistics, Disabled, live statistics without expiry, or real historical snapshots. The update interval defaults to 7 in-game days (1-365). Computers can require manual recording/CD updates or refresh automatically while powered. Renewing a paper preserves older CD snapshots. Live readers cover recorded, loaded physical compartments; newly added storage needs another inspection.

Disabled mode turns off ledger/CD/computer actions and their prerequisites, preserving saved records and physical Shopping Lists. Native transfer groups and timing, permissions, rules, priorities and presets remain unchanged.

Source-only development: no upload or native acceptance for this iteration yet. Update all peers and reconnect when testing (movement 11, Shopping 3, Personal 2, Ledger 3, Rooms 3). G7 and stable G8 remain blocked pending dedicated testing.
