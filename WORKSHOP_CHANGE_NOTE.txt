Creating a paper ledger now consumes one whole notebook. No loose pages are needed. The clipboard and pen/pencil are kept; later recording and Sort updates reuse the same ledger. Shopping List materials are unchanged.

Sort uses carried paper and a pen/pencil by default. It first searches accessible loaded building storage for a completed ledger covering the location and picks it up through normal transfer. If none is found, it uses a carried blank or unfinished Supply Sheet.

One timed writing action follows the entire successful Sort. Only confirmed touched storage is updated; blank sheets remain partial, old inspection deadlines and existing CDs are preserved. Nothing changes native transfer grouping or timing. Server SortUpdatesLedger opt-out restores the previous workflow; Disabled Ledger mode bypasses the feature.

Movement capability 12 and Ledger 5 require matching clients/server and reconnect. Source only; no package, deploy or Workshop upload. Offline checks are separate from pending native/dedicated acceptance.
