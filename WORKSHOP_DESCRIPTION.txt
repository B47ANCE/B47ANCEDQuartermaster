B47ANCE'd Quartermaster helps you organize storage, put supplies away and keep track of what you need.

Tell each container what belongs inside, then sort your inventory, a room or an entire building from one menu.

[h2]Sorting made simple[/h2]
• Choose whole categories or individual items in a searchable tree with native item icons.
• Configure several containers in one window. Save keeps the editor open so you can select the next container. Give sections their own rules or share settings across a Whole Container.
• Save and reuse sorting presets, copy settings between containers and set destination priorities.
• Sort your carried inventory, a selected container, the current room or the entire building. Room and building sorting include your carried bags.
• Sort supported floor, Proximity Inventory, selected vehicle/trailer cargo and nested carried-bag sources into configured storage. Native access and capacity checks still apply.
• Find what you need with category/item search, Only Checked, Only Contents and optional grouping by mod.
• Make quick changes with Shift-click ranges, Ctrl-click, Select All Visible, Clear All Visible and Invert Visible.

Open Quartermaster from your player inventory. Select categories to include them, expand them to choose individual items, then save your container's rules.

[h2]Shopping lists[/h2]
Create a named shopping paper from one blank sheet using a retained notebook and pen or pencil. Choose among the lists you carry, give them to friends before a loot run, or hold one in either hand for a small paper checklist HUD. Writing supplies are needed for creating/editing; reading and collecting do not need them. Previous personal plans are preserved for explicit one-time import.
• Add items manually or from supported crafting, building and cooking recipes.
• Set quantities and see what you carry and what is still missing.
• Collect remaining items from eligible nearby storage.
• Choose acceptable condition, food state and remaining uses, with preferences for individual items.

[h2]Dibs! Wanted & Needed[/h2]
Give supplies a purpose and keep your goals together.
• Add personal Dibs! labels to items.
• Keep separate Wanted and Needed lists with target quantities.
• Track the supplies you carry, add missing amounts to Shopping and collect what remains.

[h2]Building Supply Ledger[/h2]
Inventory and selected-container Sort work without a ledger. Room/Building Sort and Shopping collection require fresh location knowledge by default: carry a current completed paper supply ledger covering the location, or use a matching ledger CD loaded at an authorized powered computer in the building or nearby. A shopping paper does not replace the supply ledger. Shopping planning/reading and container configuration do not require a supply ledger; physical-list edits still need writing supplies. Server settings can disable either ledger requirement.
Computers open a Windows '93 desktop. Use CD Writer to create a ledger CD; place it in storage beside the computer, load it through CD-ROM (D:) and run QUARTERMASTER.EXE to open the application.
Craft a Supply Sheet, then walk through building or safehouse storage to record what is there, including unconfigured containers. Paper pages show rooms, containers, quantities, locations and recording times. Interrupted inspections keep completed entries.

At a powered accessible PC, copy a completed sheet onto a selected vanilla CD. The paper is kept; the CD's music is overwritten. Load the CD and run QUARTERMASTER.EXE to search names/IDs and browse rooms in the Item/Quantity/Location table with selected details. Choose periodic live statistics, Disabled, no-expiry live statistics or historical snapshots. Updates default to 7 in-game days; manual CD updates or automatic powered refresh are server settings. All powered vanilla PC orientations and placed Computer-tagged tiles qualify. The shared CRT desktop/Start menu keeps ledger, CD Writer, Minesweeper and other installed apps available. A seat directly in front supports access while resting; native acceptance remains pending.

[h2]Rooms and your base[/h2]
Use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820077442]B47ANCE'd Rooms[/url] to designate rooms and organize storage around your base. Open Designate Room from Quartermaster, then choose room or building sorting when putting supplies away.

Quartermaster checks container and room access when sorting. Room policies apply to supported mod actions; they do not lock ordinary game interactions.

For automated cleaning, add the separate [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820151540]B47ANCE'd Auto Clean[/url] mod.

[h2]Make it yours[/h2]
Resize the window, adjust the container divider and set background transparency. Mouse, keyboard and controller controls are included. Server sandbox settings control discovery range, area sorting, ledger requirements, physical lists, writing supplies and carrying allowance. Dangerous Load is optional and disabled by default; native capacity limits and encumbrance still apply.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
• Project Zomboid Build 42.21.
• Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3820077442]B47ANCE'd Rooms[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3822042011]B47ANCED PC Framework[/url]. Enable both with Quartermaster on the server and every client.

Without category mods, Quartermaster uses vanilla categories.

• Disable original Quartermaster and older B47 sorting copies.
• In multiplayer, use matching mod versions on the server and all clients.

[h2]Current limits[/h2]
Native client/dedicated acceptance is pending. CDs remain real items in accessible adjacent storage. Live statistics cover recorded, loaded storage; new storage needs inspection. Expired pages remain readable history. Selected vehicle/trailer sources are supported; automatic vehicle sweeps, vehicle destinations, fluid tanks and unverified virtual storage are not. Cooking plans use full portions; partial portions, fluids and unsupported recipes are not handled. Room policies do not lock unrelated vanilla transfers.

Room organization uses designated B47ANCEDRooms rooms only; native buildings define building scope. Retired native-room scope requests are rejected (Quartermaster Rooms capability 4). Existing building-ledger coverage and historical data are preserved. Allocation limits and live role routing remain disabled.
